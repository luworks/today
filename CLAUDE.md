# Today — iPhone planner (native app + HTML prototype)

**Today** (formerly "one.") is an AI-native planner for iPhone that puts calendar, reminders and alarms in one app. Direction: planner and health records stay local; GPT assistance sends the submitted message and bounded relevant context to the configured AI service; Apple Calendar and Apple Reminders are the source of truth (EventKit, read/write live); alarms use AlarmKit. Travel estimates send location and event addresses to Apple Maps. Weather is available only as offline sample data in Mock and Workbench; live weather is held back pending WeatherKit signing. Roadmap: **aggregate** (one place, a very good Today tab), then **optimize**, then **surpass**; match Apple Calendar / Reminders gestures and screens before improving on them. Details, rubric and eval: `docs/goal-and-rubric.md`.

The native SwiftUI app in `ios/` is the product and is ahead of the HTML/JS prototype in `src/`, which is a design reference only: fictional data, a fixed clock, no network calls. Habits were removed from both.

## Product principles (do not break these)
1. **AI prepares, the user decides.** AI never sends, moves or creates anything without an explicit tap. AI changes normally appear as a preview card (`captureCard`, `ChangeCard`) with Apply/Add and Undo. Scoped submitted-chat exception: sending a clear actionable one-off reminder request authorizes its creation directly, with no additional reminder-kind or recurrence question. Unspecified recurrence means once. Rules must establish the action and timing; ordinary chat, material ambiguity, explicit recurrence, alarms, calendar events/invites, edits and deletes retain their existing confirmation boundaries. Added appears only after the required local save and, when connected, EventKit commit succeed; retain a recoverable draft on failure and exact Undo/Open receipts on success. This does not expand any background autonomy grant. There are no automatic AI changes: nothing applies by itself, and there is no background autonomy grant. Every change, by anyone, is recorded in the Activity ledger (`Ledger`, stored in the Activity data store). Activity offers Undo for reminders, alarms, lists and one-off attendee-free event deletes while the item is unchanged and its snapshot is kept (30 days); Settle has none, and Undo never sends anything to other people (`ios/Sources/TodayCore/ActivityUndo.swift`).
2. **Meetings stay fixed by default.** Events are never moved by drag or by AI on its own. A reminder's date changes only by the user's hand or an Apply on an AI preview card; a linked alarm follows it. The one exception: the user asks the AI to move a meeting (or taps an AI conflict card), and the AI shows a preview card that says who is affected (e.g. "invitation update goes to 4 attendees"). Only the user's Apply writes it, to this occurrence only, with Undo. Details: `docs/apple-intelligence.md` §2.6.
3. **One date per reminder.** A reminder has its deadline (`due`, with or without a time of day), like Apple Reminders, and no separate "planned time" (retired October 2026). Today, Calendar, widgets, notifications and alarms all read `due`. `OneTask.plannedStart` stays in the schema, unused, so existing installs migrate; do not write or read it.
4. **Stopping an alarm ≠ finishing a task.** Only "Mark task done" completes.
5. **Ideas aren't obligations.** Notes never become tasks unless the user taps "Make a task".
6. Overdue items stay visible and are never silently moved.

## Layout: native app (`ios/`, the source of truth)
- **4 tabs: Today · Calendar · Reminders · Clock** (pills on top; swipe sideways on the top or bottom bar to switch). The top bar's right end always names the current device month, quiet and inert (`MonthLabel`, e.g. "Oct 2026"), across all tabs and Calendar modes, independently of browsed dates. It refreshes with time and foreground changes; the pills adapt or scroll before the month is crowded. Chat hides it beneath its own controls. Each tab keeps its place when you leave and come back; the tab and the Calendar view also survive a restart (not in UI tests).
- **Today** = one day (any day, from its week strip, with no month or date row of its own, or Calendar; the bottom bar's Today button always returns to today) as one timeline: an "N overdue" line on top with Tidy (opens the Tidy overdue preview), then events, reminders at their due time, alarms and quiet "Free 1 h 30 min" rows; reminders due that day with no time sit under "Anytime today". On today a quiet health line (steps + sleep by default, Settings → Health summary) opens the **Health sheet**, a compact grouped list of metrics the user picks (Manage Metrics with visibility toggles, Hide, reorder; default steps, sleep, weight; row = value + date label + 7-day sparkline, tap for a 7/30/90 day chart; read-only; the sheet, each metric detail, Manage Metrics and Settings → Today have an **Open Health** button into Apple's Health app, `HealthLauncher`, `x-apple-health://`; `HealthMetrics.swift`, `docs/health/metrics-and-density.md`); Tomorrow is viewed by selecting its day in the week strip or Calendar, not through a timeline disclosure; **Calendar** = 2 Day (default: a time grid of two days, today first or the day picked in Month/Year; a swipe moves one day; events are blocks, staggered where they overlap; timed reminders and alarms are one-line chips at their time, extra ones on a crowded hour fold into "+N") / Month / Year modeled on Apple Calendar, Day = the Today tab; which calendars show is a Setting; past days are dimmed (Setting "Dim past days", on) and weekends have no tint or special colour; first day of the week follows the region unless Settings picks Sunday/Monday (`Calendar.app`); optional week numbers (Month rows; "W43" over the hours in 2 Day, the week of the first column); "Open Calendar on" picks the view at launch. **Reminders** = modeled on Apple Reminders: small pills (wrapping, no cards, no empty band) for the smart lists Today/Scheduled/All/Flagged/Completed (with counts) and pinned lists, kept above the List so each pill has its own long-press menu, My Lists (color + symbol, create/rename/delete, reorder, pin), no Edit button or edit mode anywhere (long-press menu: Rename / Color & Icon / Pin / Delete; press-hold-drag to reorder; trailing swipe via `RowAction`; which smart lists show and their order is in Settings), list screens with an inline New Reminder row (note, date, flag, repeat, suggestions). Two-way sync with Apple Reminders via EventKit (`RemindersSync`, Apple wins on conflict). **Clock** (clock symbol; formerly the Alarms tab) = modeled on Apple's Clock app: real alarms (`OneAlarm`, AlarmKit) plus items marked "Ring as alarm", split by a quiet switcher into Alarms · World Clock (Overlap-style: home first, time-of-day cards, sideways scrub, overlap chart) · Stopwatch · Timer (Apple-style Timers: hours/minutes/seconds wheels, up to 23 h 59 min, Label/Sound, Recents and running timer rings; Focus and breaks remain as three preset Recents); no nag-until-done reminders. **Running timers show at the top of the Today tab** (up to 2 live countdown rows + "+N more"; Pause/Resume/Stop by swipe, tap opens the Timer tab). Today only sees timers it started: timers from the Clock app or Siri's built-in "set a timer" are not readable by any app (`docs/research/timer-siri-sync.md`), so the Timer tab says so.
- **Alarm time** in the alarm editor is the custom wheel from the alarm wheel lab, version C (`TimeWheelPicker`): minutes in ten-minute steps (00, 10 … 50); an existing alarm that is not on a ten-minute mark keeps every-minute rows, so opening and saving it never moves its minute. A new alarm opens on the next ten-minute mark (`AlarmPlan.nextTenMinutes`).
- **Settings** (sidebar) with a compact Appearance menu row, then sections: Apple Calendar/Reminders/Alarms; Today (alarms, scroll to now, free time, travel time, Health); Calendar (dim past days, first day of week, week numbers, open on); Reminders ("New reminders go to" Auto or a list, Smart Lists); Alarms (new-alarm snooze and sound); AI (Ask suggestions, archive chats); Activity data; Delete All Data. New choices live in `ios/Sources/TodayCore/Preferences.swift` (keys + defaults = the old behaviour, unit-tested) with rows in `SettingsRows.swift`; add a choice only if it changes what the user sees or how the app acts, with the current behaviour as the default.
- **Top-left is a menu button** (three lines) that opens the **sidebar**, like ChatGPT/Claude (a swipe from the left edge does too): Search at the top, past AI chats grouped by day (swipe Archive/Delete, long-press Rename, an Archived section; finished chats auto-archive, Settings → "Archive finished chats"), New chat and Settings at the bottom. Search lives only in the sidebar. **Settings** starts with **Appearance** (a compact row showing the current System / Light / Dark choice with a native menu; default System; `@AppStorage` `today.appearance`, applied at the root with `preferredColorScheme` and to every window's `overrideUserInterfaceStyle` so Apple's event editor matches; widgets and Lock Screen follow the system).
- **Bottom bar**: an "Ask AI or add anything" chat field that fills the bar, then a "Today" text button (a capsule as tall as **+**; from any tab or another day it shows today itself) and **+** on the right. The field opens a new chat every time as a full-screen page like ChatGPT (menu to past chats top-left, Close top-right back to the page, composer at the bottom with the keyboard up; Reminder / Event / Alarm buttons above it select a kind for the next message, highlighted, without touching the draft; on send the kind's small skill prompt ("Remind me to " / "Add an event " / "Set an alarm for ") goes ahead of the typed text so the AI knows the kind, while the chat shows only what was typed; iterate a kind by editing its `skill`, `AskQuickKind`). The next new chat is built hidden ahead of time (after launch and after each Close) and the keyboard is warmed at launch, so a tap only reveals it, the first tap included. Closing keeps the conversation in the sidebar from its first message; an unsent draft is dropped. A chat opened from the sidebar closes back to the sidebar. The **+** opens the same menu on every page: New alarm / New reminder / New event (plus Add city on World Clock). A new reminder opens the Due-style quick add (`QuickReminderSheet`: one-tap time grid, guesses from your own past reminders, `ReminderSuggest`); a new event uses Apple's own editor when Calendar access is on.
- **Gestures follow Apple**: tap the circle to complete; swipe right-to-left for actions, the same look everywhere (`RowActions`: Delete red and rightmost, full swipe deletes with an Undo toast; deleting a reminder, alarm or one-off event never asks first (Undo is the safety net); Flag, Archive, Pin); long-press menu (Ask AI first, then Show Details / Flag / Today / Tomorrow / Unschedule / Delete; Ask AI (also on Settled rows) opens a new chat about that item, `AskSubject`: "it" means the item and the model is told its title and facts); a repeating event asks "this event or the series?" before Delete; Apple Calendar events open in Apple's own viewer; shake to undo. Nothing is moved by drag in the 2 Day grid; events are fixed and a reminder's date changes from its menu or editor.
- **AI**: rules first (`CaptureParser`), with the hosted Luna model (`gpt-6-luna` behind `backend/luna`) for selected operations; there is no Foundation Models call on main. The target design, a single on-phone agent loop with typed tools and code-owned writes, is `docs/engineering/ai-agent-architecture.md`. One line can propose several items at once (`CaptureStack`); follow-ups edit the preview; edits to existing items ("move the dentist to 4") show a ChangeCard. "Tidy overdue" suggests a new date per overdue reminder (`OverdueTidy`; each row changeable, Apply writes the reminder date keeping its time, Undo). Except for the scoped clear one-off submitted-chat request above, nothing is written until Add/Apply. A capture card stays in the chat after it is decided (`CardStatus`: Added with Undo and Open, Dropped, Undone with Add again; decided cards are saved with the thread, `StoredCard`), under the AI message that proposed it. The empty Ask page shows 2-3 suggestion chips that are guesses from the user's own data (`AskSuggestions.swift`: rule candidates, each a full sentence that is checked to route to something actionable via `SentenceRoute`; the hosted model only re-ranks and rewords them, `AskSuggestionRanker`, 2 s cap, rules otherwise). Tests that need a particular action type the sentence instead of tapping a chip.
- **Widgets** (`ios/TodayWidgets`): Next Up on the Lock Screen and Home Screen, fed by a JSON snapshot in the App Group (`WidgetFeed`). Lock Screen rectangular shows the next item only (time line + live timer + "+N", title on two lines; `ios/Sources/TodayShared/LockScreenNext.swift`); Home Screen small/medium keep their lists.
- **Share extension** (`ios/ShareExtension`): Share → Today turns text or a Safari page into an editable New Reminder with an optional day; Add puts it in an App Group inbox (`ios/Sources/TodayShared/ShareInbox.swift`) that the app imports when it becomes active, with an Undo toast.
- Habits are gone (no tab, chips or streaks). The `Habit` SwiftData model stays in the schema, unused, so existing installs migrate.
- See `ios/README.md` for files, the test dataset, widgets and how to run on a phone.

## Layout: HTML prototype (`src/`)
- **Home (`ui.tab==='today'`, `vHome`)**: calendar pane on top (week strip, next item, due-today tray, timeline) + resizable split handle (peek / half / full) + conversation pane below. With no messages the calendar pane fills the screen (just the items: one quiet "N overdue" line, the overlap banner, due-today tray, timeline); the split handle and conversation pane appear with the first message. There is no AI brief card (removed on purpose: the page shows items, AI lives in the composer).
- **Composer**: always-visible bottom input (`#ask`). Statements are parsed into a capture card (`parse` → `captureCard`); questions go to `aiReply` (scripted). `isQuestion` decides.
- **Side drawer** (`renderDrawer`): ☰ button or left-edge swipe. Today / Calendar / Reminders / Notes / Alarms (no live summaries), conversations (`threads`), AI activity, Lock Screen & widgets mock, Settings.
- Secondary full views: `vCalendar` (week/month only; the day view is Home), `vTasks` (shown as "Reminders": tasks/projects/notes), `vAlarms` (real alarms only; reminders are a per-item alert, not a list).
- Sheets with detents (`openSheet`/`renderSheet`), iOS action sheets (`openMenu`), ring screen (`showRing`), toasts with Undo.
- `vToday` is the older list-style Today; it is no longer routed but kept for reference.

## Workflow

Use `./dev` from the repository root as the sole local/CI engineering entry. Start with `./dev doctor`, then `./dev bootstrap`, `./dev generate` and `./dev resolve`. Build with `./dev build Debug`, run logic with `./dev test unit`, and select focused UI methods with `./dev test ui --only TodayUITests/<Suite>/<Method>`. The [iOS guide](ios/README.md) owns command examples, pinned tools, resource ownership and troubleshooting; [architecture](docs/engineering/architecture.md) owns module boundaries. Existing eval/preview scripts are underlying adapters or historical evidence, not parallel instructions. The HTML prototype remains a design reference; consult `./dev help` for supported engineering operations.

## Conventions / gotchas
iOS app:
- `-uiTesting` = fresh in-memory store; add `-dataset <path>` to load the test dataset (debug builds only; Apple Calendar / Reminders sync stays off). UI tests use `TestData.launchArguments`; the tests that need Apple Calendar pass `-uiTestingCalendarSync`.
- Logic that can be unit-tested lives in plain Swift with no SwiftUI/SwiftData (`Logic.swift`, `ConversationHistory.swift`, `Dialogue.swift`, `QuickTime.swift`, `TimerDial.swift`, `ios/Sources/TodayShared/WidgetSnapshot.swift`) in linked production targets and runs through `./dev test unit` (tests in `ios/Eval/Tests/OneLogicTests`).
- Swipe actions come from `RowActions.swift` so every list looks the same; reminder changes go through `TaskActions` (Undo toast + shake to undo), list changes through `ListActions`.
- Appearance: colors must be adaptive (`Color(.label)`, `.primary`, `systemBackground`, ...); white is only for text on a colored fill. Under `-uiTesting` the choice starts on System; `-appearance light|dark` sets it, `-uiTestingKeepAppearance` keeps what the last launch saved. Logic in `AppearanceLogic.swift` (unit-tested), UI in `Appearance.swift`.
- Settings → Delete All Data never touches Apple Calendar or Apple Reminders (the sync link is forgotten first).
- Activity data store (`ActivityStore`, an app-private SQLite file; details in `ios/README.md`, reasons in `docs/research/activity-data-store.md`): the ledger and every recorded AI/usage/system event live there, not in SwiftData (`LedgerEntry` stays in the schema, unused). Only the app process opens the file; extensions append JSONL to the App Group `activity-outbox/` (`ios/Sources/TodayShared/ActivityOutbox.swift`). Record through `Ledger.record` (changes) or `ActivityCenter.record(ActivityEvents.…)`; `props` never holds free text (titles, diffs and chat text go in their own columns/tables); page reads with the `(ts, id)` cursor, never load all rows. Raw events default to Forever; explicit shorter retention, Pause, per-category switches, export and Clear are in Settings → Activity data; Pure parts live in TodayData and run through `./dev test unit`.

HTML prototype:
- Every state change goes through `commit(label, fn)` so it is undoable and persisted; then `render(true)` keeps scroll positions.
- UI actions are `data-act="..."` attributes dispatched through the `A` map; add new actions there.
- Never use `alert/confirm/prompt` (the claude.ai artifact frame blocks them). Use `openMenu('confirm-…')`.
- Touch drag on iOS must use touch events (see the `TD` handler); pointer events get cancelled when the DOM under the finger is replaced. Don't re-render the dragged element mid-drag.
- Left-edge swipe conflicts with Safari's back gesture; it works from the Home Screen (standalone) and in a native app.
- `localStorage` key `one-v8`; Settings → Reset sample data restores the seed.
- Debug handle: `window.oneV8` (`data`, `go(tab)`, `openDrawer()`, `A`, `parse`, `needs`, `sendAI`, `reset`).

## Where it's going
1. **Aggregate** (done): Reminders tab like Apple Reminders with two-way EventKit sync (complete, rename, delete and repeat tested both ways), Calendar via EventKit, alarms, world clock, stopwatch and a focus timer via AlarmKit.
2. **Optimize** (in progress): AI capture and edit (moving to the agent architecture) and "Tidy overdue", always as a preview the user confirms; Lock Screen / Home Screen widgets with background refresh; Live Activities for the timer and snoozing alarms; Siri / Shortcuts (App Intents); a Share extension. Next: a full eval run on the rubric.
3. **Surpass**: improve on Apple's screens only after parity is demonstrated.

The prototype is not kept in lockstep; it will not get EventKit, AlarmKit or a live model (`aiReply` stays scripted).
